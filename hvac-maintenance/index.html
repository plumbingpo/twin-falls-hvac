Warning Signs for HVAC Maintenance

Spotting the early warning signs gives you the chance to act before the situation spirals into something far more expensive. These are the signals our technicians see most often when a problem is underway:

  • Musty air when the fan starts, or a filter that has been in far too long
  • Cooling or heating that takes noticeably longer to reach the setpoint
  • A breakdown last season that arrived with zero warning
  • A startup sound that has changed since last year
  • Nobody has opened up the system in over a year
  • No real sense of how healthy your heating and cooling equipment is
  • Recently installed equipment whose manufacturer expects maintenance records
  • Energy bills creeping up without any change in your habits

The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

There is a short list of things homeowners can handle: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and checking the drain. HVAC Maintenance Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Schedule maintenance in early spring and early fall, ahead of the busy season and the extreme weather
  • Flush the condensate line every spring so algae cannot build up and clog it
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Note new sounds and longer run times; they are data your technician can use
  • Leave every supply and return register open, including those in unused rooms
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of vegetation and rinse the coil gently each spring
  • Change the filter every one to three months; it is the single highest-value habit there is
